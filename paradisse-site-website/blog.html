What is a Russian manicure, and do you offer it?

Yes, it is our specialty: dry, meticulous cuticle work with fine scissors and nippers so gel sits flush to the cuticle and lasts longer. More on manicures.

How much does a gel manicure cost?

A gel manicure is $55 and a regular polish manicure is $25, cream massage and warm towel wrap included. Full price list.

How long does a gel manicure last?

Two to three weeks with good prep and aftercare; cuticle oil and gloves for dishes stretch it further. Aftercare tips.

Where are you located and how do I book?

60 King Rd, Unit 202, Richmond Hill (Oak Ridges). Book online on Vagaro or text 365 553 8902. Visiting the studio.
