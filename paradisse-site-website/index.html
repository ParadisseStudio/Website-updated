Our Story

Where beauty
meets care

Paradisse Studio is a beauty and wellness studio in Richmond Hill, Ontario, offering manicures, pedicures, facials, lashes, and a range of professional beauty services in a calm, refined, and welcoming setting.

We are dedicated to helping every client feel relaxed, confident, and beautifully cared for through quality service, attention to detail, and a personalized experience with every visit.

The Team

Small team, big care

Every visit to Paradisse is looked after by one of three people who genuinely love what they do.

S

Sofiia

Russian Manicure Specialist

With more than five years of experience, Sofiia specializes in Russian manicures and meticulous deep cuticle work, using fine cuticle scissors and nippers for a clean, precise finish that lasts. She loves nail art and brings the same careful attention to detail to every set and pedicure.

L

Leslie

Pedicures, Spray Tans & Lash Lifts

Leslie is our pedicure specialist, a certified spray tan artist and a certified lash lift technician. She is also the friendly face who books your appointments and takes care of you at checkout, keeping the studio running smoothly from your first hello to your last.
