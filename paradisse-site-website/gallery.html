About this work

What you're looking at

Most of the sets in this gallery start with a Russian manicure: dry, meticulous cuticle work with fine scissors and nippers, so the gel sits flush against the skin and grows out clean for two to three weeks. From there it's your call, from a quiet milky white to hand-painted florals, polka dots, chrome French tips or a shimmering cat-eye.

Prices for everything you see are on our services page: a gel manicure is $55, gel extensions are $65, and nail art starts at $5 per design. Not sure what suits your hands? Our guide to choosing a nail shape and our FAQ are good places to start.

Popular looks in this gallery

  • Milky white and sheer nude gel on almond and square nails, the studio's most requested everyday finish.
  • French tips reimagined: classic white, silver chrome, emerald green, red with black outlines and sunset ombre.
  • Hand-painted nail art, from fruit vines and holly to florals, polka dots and character accents.
  • Cat-eye and chrome finishes in champagne, rose and pearl for a soft, light-catching shimmer.
  • Statement shapes: long almond, stiletto and gel extensions built for length and strength.
