Why Paradisse

Why women in Richmond Hill keep coming back

There are plenty of nail salons along Yonge Street. Here is what makes an hour at Paradisse feel different.

01

Russian manicure, done properly

Both Angely and Sofiia specialize in dry, deep cuticle work using fine cuticle scissors and nippers. The result is gel that sits right at the cuticle, grows out cleanly and lasts weeks instead of days.

02

Immaculate prep, spotless studio

Our clients mention two things again and again: the prep and the cleanliness. We take our time on both, because a manicure only looks as good as the foundation underneath it.

03

A small team you actually know

Angely, Sofiia and Leslie are the whole team. No rotating strangers, no rushing you out for the next chair. We remember your shape, your shade and how you like your coffee.

The little rituals

Cream massage and a warm towel wrap come standard with every manicure and pedicure. It is a small thing, but it is the reason so many clients call this their favourite hour of the week.

Good to know

Questions we hear from Richmond Hill clients

Where is Paradisse Studio located in Richmond Hill?

We are at 60 King Rd, Unit 202, Richmond Hill, ON L4E 1A3, in the Oak Ridges neighbourhood just off Yonge Street. We are a short drive from Lake Wilcox, Jefferson, King City and Aurora. Get directions.

Do you offer Russian manicures in Richmond Hill?

Yes. Russian (dry) manicures are our specialty. Angely and Sofiia are trained in detailed cuticle work using fine scissors and nippers, so the gel sits close to the cuticle for a clean look that grows out beautifully.

How do I book an appointment?

Book online anytime through Vagaro, or call or text 365 553 8902 during studio hours.

Which areas near Richmond Hill do you serve?

Clients visit us from across Richmond Hill, including Oak Ridges, Lake Wilcox, Jefferson, Bond Lake, Mill Pond and Richmond Hill Centre, as well as King City, Aurora, Newmarket, Vaughan, Markham and Thornhill.

How long does a gel manicure last?

With careful prep and good aftercare, a gel manicure from Paradisse Studio typically lasts two to three weeks without chipping. Daily cuticle oil and gloves for dishes help it last even longer. Read our aftercare tips.

Do you offer lash and spray tan services too?

Yes. Alongside nails we offer classic lash extensions, lash lifts, eyebrow shaping, tinting and waxing, and custom airbrush spray tans, all in the same Richmond Hill studio. See all services and prices.
